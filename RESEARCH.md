## Recommendation: **GodDot (Dname)**
Why: (1) Highest views per video of the five creators (median 1.7M). (2) His content already is game design: we turn
his video formats into playable web games, which is our strength, needs no webcam, ML or paid APIs, and is original IP
with no Brawl Stars or Mojang branding. (3) "I leave it up to you" plus a non-obvious channel probably means fewer
competing teams than Kkwallu, whose suggested "mini-games hub" will attract every game team **[UNVERIFIED guess]**.
(4) "Subscribe and join the game" means fans already want to play with him. We give them a place to do it between videos.
Risks: rare uploads, a minor audience (payments → parents/vouchers/ads), and no public demographics.
Prize events must stay free-entry and sponsor-funded so they don't count as gambling or lotteries (Korean law).
Korea's rating rule for online games (GRAC) needs a check before commercial launch.

### Concept A: "디네임 챌린지 아레나" (Dname Challenge Arena), top pick
- Core loop: a weekly "episode" of 3 to 5 quick browser party games straight from his formats: Find the Diamond (grid-dig luck),
  Greed Game (take less than your neighbor), Block Telepathy (match the crowd's pick), Fake Lava (spot the fake),
  and a Don't-Say-ㄹ word game. Real-time rooms of 10 to 100 players (WebSocket) or async vs. ghost scores.
  A season leaderboard and a weekly "final" streamed and recorded by Dname, whose clips become his next short
  (content flywheel: game → video → game).
- Money: free to play. Cosmetic blocky skins/emotes (₩1,000 to 5,000), a ₩4,900/month "fan pass" (extra lives, private rooms
  for friend groups, badge), sponsor-branded episodes (food/snack brands already in his niche: chicken, lava, emerald).
  Prizes come from the sponsor, never from players. Kid-safe ads on non-pass plays.
- Why these fans: the same games they watch at 2M views, now playable with Dname, with a real chance to appear in his video.

### Concept B: "눈치게임 TV" (crowd-psychology party game for classrooms and friend groups)
- Core loop: Jackbox-style. One screen hosts and phones join with a 4-letter code. Rounds of balance games, telepathy,
  the trust/greed dilemma and roulette. Dname voice lines and stickers.
- Money: free basic pack. ₩3,900 to 9,900 one-time theme packs; creator-made weekly question packs on subscription;
  school/academy group license. Jackbox proves people pay for this.
- Why: his top formats (balance game, telepathy, trust) are social games kids play in class and on Discord.

### Concept C: "Dname Lucky Mine" (daily one-minute puzzle, Wordle-style)
- One shared daily dig/minesweeper-like puzzle ("find the diamond in 1 minute") with a shareable emoji result,
  streaks and a friend league. Cheap to build, and the Wordle share loop drives growth.
- Money: ads plus ₩2,900/month for an ad-free archive and hint tokens. Sponsored "golden block" days.
  This is weaker on the business model, so use it as a retention feature inside A.

## Trends: why now (sources checked 2026-11-06)
- **Korean kids live on short-form.** Korea Press Foundation's "2025 Tweens' and Teens' Media Use" (2,674 students from grade 4 to grade 12,
  fieldwork by Korea Gallup, released 2026-01-07) found 200.6 min of online video a day (elementary 143.6 min, middle school 233.7 min).
  **49.1% watch short-form every day** (0.2% in the 2022 survey). Most-used platforms: Instagram Reels 37.2%, YouTube 35.8%, YouTube Shorts 16.5%.
  Source: Korea Times, 2026-01-07, https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/southkorea/society/20260107/korean-teens-watch-online-videos-3-hours-daily-half-view-short-form-content-every-day
- **YouTube Shorts dominates short-form across ages:** 93.4% of Korean short-form users use it (KOCCA 2025 Content Usage Survey,
  6,554 people aged 10+). Source: https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2025121510582925254 (Asia Economy, Dec 2025; summary, not the primary report).
- **Platforms are pushing "play with your audience".** In Sep 2025 YouTube added Playables minigames to Live: 75+ casual games that the
  *creator* plays while chatting. It also tested a live leaderboard of the top 50 participating viewers.
  Source: TechCrunch 2025-09-16, https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/16/youtube-live-gets-a-major-update-introduces-dual-format-streaming-minigames-and-more/
  → Our angle is the step after that. With Playables only the creator plays. In Block Party, **every viewer plays on their own phone**, in the creator's own formats.
- **Daily shared puzzles with a share grid** (the Wordle pattern) are now a standard retention loop. Our Daily Challenge uses it to turn
  one-off Shorts views into a daily habit. (No figures claimed.)
